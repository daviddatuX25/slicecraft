# SliceCraft Image Generation Style Bible

Apply this direction to every combination prompt.

## Overall look

Create an original premium bakery product photograph for the fictional SliceCraft cake shop. The image should feel warm, handmade, polished, and celebratory while remaining clean enough for a modern admin product preview.

## Composition

- One whole cake as the hero subject.
- Three-quarter front view, centered.
- Cake occupies approximately 65–75% of the frame.
- Keep the silhouette and toppings clearly visible.
- Use a simple warm cream studio background.
- Add a soft natural contact shadow.
- Leave a small amount of breathing room around the cake.
- Use a consistent 4:5 portrait composition.

## Lighting and color

- Soft diffused bakery-studio lighting.
- Warm neutral highlights.
- Realistic frosting texture.
- Natural food color; avoid neon or plastic-looking colors.
- Keep the image bright enough for a card preview.

## Do not include

- Brand names or logos.
- Cake2Go packaging, labels, or copied decorations.
- Watermarks.
- Text rendered into the image.
- People, hands, forks, plates with logos, or busy backgrounds.
- Multiple cakes unless a prompt explicitly requests a single garnish arrangement.
- Impossible frosting geometry or melted-looking toppings.

## Reference-image rule

Use the selected Cake2Go image only for broad inspiration about the flavor's visual identity and product-photo framing. Do not reproduce its exact arrangement, decorations, camera crop, or branding.

Use additive references only for the named additive. Combine them into a new SliceCraft-specific composition.

## Output quality checklist

- [ ] One cake only.
- [ ] Correct base flavor/color.
- [ ] Correct size presentation, including Bento when specified.
- [ ] Correct named topping/decorations.
- [ ] No visible text or logo.
- [ ] Clean warm background.
- [ ] Product-card-ready portrait composition.
- [ ] Original composition rather than a copy of a source image.
