# Standalone Additive Reference Library

This folder contains isolated references attached to image-generation prompts when building combinations.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Anti-Contamination Rule:**
> When attaching an additive reference to a combination prompt, the generator must isolate **only the specific decorative object or piping pattern**. Never copy the underlying cake body, background props, plates, or colors from the reference photo.

---

## Additive Isolation Specifications & Notes

### 1. Fresh Fruit Slices (`fresh-fruit-reference-01.png`)
* **🎯 Target Object:** Fresh vibrant sliced strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, and small fresh mint sprigs.
* **🚫 Ignore / Exclude:** Do NOT copy the flat plate or circular plate arrangement.
* **🎂 Application Pattern:** Arranged in a tight, natural cluster on the top center or an elegant crescent on one side of the cake.

### 2. Mixed Berries (`mixed-berries-reference-01.png`)
* **🎯 Target Object:** Dewy, plump mixed forest berries (strawberries, blackberries, blueberries, raspberries).
* **🚫 Ignore / Exclude:** Do NOT copy the serving surface or tableware.
* **🎂 Application Pattern:** Natural berry crown placed on top of the cake, preserving visible frosting underneath.

### 3. Chocolate Ganache Drip (`chocolate-drip-reference-01.png`)
* **🎯 Target Object:** Glossy, viscous dark chocolate ganache drips running cleanly down the rim edge.
* **🚫 Ignore / Exclude:** **CRITICAL:** Do NOT copy the ivory cake cylinder, cake height, or cake flavor from this photo. Only extract the liquid drip geometry and sheen.
* **🎂 Application Pattern:** Applied strictly around the top edge circumference of the base cake, dripping naturally 1/3 to 1/2 way down the sides.

### 4. Toasted Coconut Flakes (`coconut-garnish-reference-01.png`)
* **🎯 Target Object:** Fluffy, golden-brown toasted shredded coconut flakes.
* **🚫 Ignore / Exclude:** Do NOT copy the ceramic plate, scoop, wooden spoon, or burlap cloth.
* **🎂 Application Pattern:** Light, tasteful dusting along the top outer rim and/or lower base perimeter. Do not smother the cake.

### 5. Bakery Macarons (`macarons-reference-01.png`)
* **🎯 Target Object:** 3 to 5 delicate pastel French macarons with smooth feet and rich filling (vanilla cream, cocoa, soft berry).
* **🚫 Ignore / Exclude:** Do NOT copy the ceramic plate, wooden table, linen napkin, or background pitcher.
* **🎂 Application Pattern:** Nestled securely on the top surface of the cake, tilted gracefully against piped rosettes or toppings.

### 6. Dark Chocolate Curls (`chocolate-curls-reference-01.png`)
* **🎯 Target Object:** Thin, spiral-rolled dark chocolate shavings and curls.
* **🚫 Ignore / Exclude:** Do NOT copy the ceramic bowl, spoon, spilled cocoa, or rustic tabletop.
* **🎂 Application Pattern:** Gently piled in the center top as a light textured nest, or sprinkled across the surface.

### 7. Buttercream Floral Piping (`floral-decoration-reference-01.png`)
* **🎯 Target Object:** Hand-piped edible buttercream flowers (soft ivory roses, pale lavender petals, delicate green leaves).
* **🚫 Ignore / Exclude:** Do NOT copy the ceramic serving dish or background wood grain.
* **🎂 Application Pattern:** Arranged as a botanical bouquet cluster on one quadrant of the cake top or cascading softly down one side.

### 8. Celebration Rosette Border Trim (`celebration-piping-reference-01.png`)
* **🎯 Target Object:** Vintage ornate pastry piping: piped top shell borders, rosette swirls, and swag garlands.
* **🚫 Ignore / Exclude:** **CRITICAL:** Do NOT copy the underlying cake body, height, ivory color, or pastry tools. Only extract the border piping motif.
* **🎂 Application Pattern:** The decorative piping borders are applied to the top rim and bottom base of the designated cake, adopting the base cake's flavor-appropriate color scheme (e.g. lavender for Ube, cocoa for Fudge, ivory/peach for Mango).

### 9. Bento Cake Proportions (`bento-cake-proportion-reference-01.png`)
* **🎯 Target Object:** Scale and proportions only: petite 4-inch mini-cake diameter, cute compact height ratio (serves 4–6).
* **🚫 Ignore / Exclude:** Do NOT copy the peach color, the specific heart topper, the paper liner, or table props.
* **🎂 Application Pattern:** Rescales the chosen base cake into a petite bento format while strictly keeping the base cake's authentic flavor, frosting color, and texture.

### 10. Smooth Cream Frosting Texture (`cream-frosting-texture-reference-01.png`)
* **🎯 Target Object:** Velvet-smooth frosting finish with subtle spatula swooshes and creamy bakery luster.
* **🚫 Ignore / Exclude:** Do NOT copy spatulas or utensils.
* **🎂 Application Pattern:** Baseline texture applied to all frosting surfaces across every cake model.
